FreePlayer

An IPTV player for LG TVs

Watch the live TV service you already subscribe to, on your LG webOS TV.

Features

  • Connect an M3U playlist URL or an Xtream Codes login
  • Browse channels by group, with favorites and search
  • TV guide (XMLTV) with now/next info and a full programme grid
  • Built for the TV remote and the Magic Remote pointer
FreePlayer does not provide any content. It doesn't include, sell or recommend channels or playlists. You need your own subscription or playlist, and you should only use services you are legally entitled to access.

Try it with the demo playlist

To see how the app works, enter this playlist URL on the FreePlayer setup screen (M3U playlist URL tab, EPG field left empty):

demo/playlist.m3u
